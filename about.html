ABOUT LONGEVAL NETWORKS

Your trusted partner in network solutions.

Delivering Excellence in Network Rentals Since 2010

Established in 2010 and based in New Delhi, Longeval Networks is a leading provider of Refurbished Switch, Router, and Firewall rental services across India. With a strong reputation for high-quality service and customer satisfaction, we are proud to be a trusted partner to businesses of all sizes, offering reliable, scalable, and cost-effective networking solutions.

Expert Refurbished Solutions

We specialize in offering Refurbished Network Equipment rentals that cater to the diverse needs of our clients. From enterprise-grade switches and routers to secure firewalls and data center gear, every unit we provide is rigorously tested to meet the highest standards of performance, reliability, and value.

Nationwide Coverage & Reliable Support

With a pan-India presence, we provide nationwide delivery, installation, and 24/7 technical support, ensuring your network infrastructure remains operational without interruption. Our commitment lies in delivering quality service and dependable refurbished equipment that meets your business needs — every time.
