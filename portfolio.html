About Ravindra Singh

Network Infrastructure Specialist | Business Consultant | CCIE Service Provider

With over two decades of distinguished experience in enterprise network infrastructure and telecommunications, I bring a unique combination of deep technical expertise and strategic business acumen to every engagement. As a certified CCIE Service Provider (#38999), I have architected, implemented, and optimized mission-critical network solutions for Fortune 500 companies, global telecommunications operators, and enterprises across multiple continents.

Following my extensive tenure at Aircel Ltd. as Assistant Manager of NOC IP/MPLS Core & Managed Services, I transitioned into entrepreneurship, leveraging my technical foundation to provide comprehensive business consulting and network infrastructure advisory services. My journey from hands-on network engineering to strategic business leadership positions me uniquely to understand both the technical intricacies and business implications of infrastructure decisions.

Today, as a business consultant and entrepreneur, I help organizations navigate the complex landscape of digital transformation, network modernization, and IT infrastructure optimization. My approach combines technical depth with business strategy, ensuring that technology investments align with organizational goals and deliver measurable ROI.
